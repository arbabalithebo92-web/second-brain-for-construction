The INDEX doc (the secret)

Paste this into "00 - INDEX". Ask your AI assistant to read it at the start of every chat.

00 - INDEX  (read this first)

WHO I AM: [Name], [role], [city]. Language: [English / Urdu].

RULES FOR THE ASSISTANT
1. Read this INDEX first, then the project's "00 - STATUS" doc.
2. After any work, update that STATUS doc: done / next / waiting on.
3. New files go into the right folder, never into the root.
4. Ask me before sending anything to a client.

ACTIVE PROJECTS
- Client - 10 Marla House BOQ  → status: BOQ draft sent, waiting rates
- Tender - School Building PQ  → status: experience letters pending

ONGOING AREAS
- Material tracking, site expenses, marketing

LAST UPDATED: [date]

How it works every day

Drop any file or note into 00 - INBOX (drawing from WhatsApp, bill photo, voice note).
Once a day, move it to the right Project or Area. Or ask your AI assistant to do it.
Start every AI chat with: "Read my INDEX and the STATUS of [project]." No more explaining from zero.
When a job ends, move its folder to 04 - ARCHIVE. Your history stays searchable.
